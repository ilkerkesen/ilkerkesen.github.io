I am a postdoc at [the Machine Learning chair](https://alanakbik.github.io/chair) at the Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. Previously, I was a postdoc at [Language and Multimodal Processing Group](https://lampgroup.github.io/) at the University of Copenhagen. I completed my PhD at Koç University, under the supervision of [Deniz Yuret](https://www.denizyuret.com). My main research area is natural language processing (NLP). In particular, I'm currently working on tokenization-free approaches and less-resourced languages. The easiest way to contact me is to [send an email](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
